Business Workflows & Flowcharts

How the business flows through the Follow-Up System — the operating loop, each process flow, and the state machines that govern lifecycles.

Legend: rounded blue = process/action · amber diamond = decision · dark-blue stadium = start/end · amber = external system/actor · green edges = "yes/success", red edges = "no/reject".

1 · The end-to-end operating loop

Everything the system does serves one repeating value chain: acquire and manage client labs, collect from them daily, move and receive the samples, serve and retain the relationship, measure and pay, and integrate with the upstream system.

2 · Daily collection cycle (swimlane)

The core daily process, from automated board generation through collection, transfer, receipt, and monthly roll-up. Roles are shown as swimlanes.

2.1 Rules that govern the cycle

  • A check-in records a sample count (a suggested value is offered), may auto-create an outsource row, and auto-updates the lab's derived status.
  • An undo is refused once a visit is collected/transferred.
  • Every action is organizational-scope-checked, audited, and broadcasts a real-time refetch signal to connected clients.
  • Still-Pending visits are swept to Missed in the evening (see the build-correction in FR-5) before the midnight archive.
